# NOVA GUILD Campaign

This context describes the fictional NOVA GUILD newcomer campaign represented by the landing-page hero.

## Language

**Guild Festa**:
The limited-time newcomer campaign branded as “SUNLIT GUILD FESTA.”
_Avoid_: Event, sale

**Adventurer**:
A visitor invited to become a member of the guild through the Guild Festa.
_Avoid_: User, player, customer

**Entry**:
The Adventurer’s primary response to the Guild Festa invitation.
_Avoid_: Registration, purchase

## Characters

**Rocko**:
The crystal-bearing stone Adventurer who appears as part of the Guild Festa party.
_Avoid_: Rock character, stone monster
